🚀 KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

✅ Card Payment Processing
   - Process payments on Moniepoint POS terminals
   - Method: MoniepointPosSdk.makeCardPayment(amount)

✅ Receipt Printing
   - Print formatted receipts with QR codes
   - Support for 6 receipt types (TITLE, KEY_VALUE, ITEMS, QR, etc.)
   - Method: MoniepointPosSdk.printReceipt(items)

✅ Terminal Data Access
   - Get terminal ID, serial number, and model
   - Method: MoniepointPosSdk.getTerminalData()

✅ Secure Credential Management
   - Credentials in gradle.properties (not in code)
   - Users configure their own Maven credentials

✅ Full TypeScript Support
   - Complete type definitions
   - IntelliSense support in all IDEs
